PETA WARISAN KAMPOENG HERITAGE KAJOETANGAN

Sejarah Kampoeng Heritage Kayutangan

Kayutangan merupakan salah satu kawasan perdagangan penting di Kota Malang yang berkembang sejak masa kolonial Belanda. Di balik koridor perdagangan utamanya, terdapat permukiman Kampoeng Kayutangan yang menjadi ruang kehidupan sosial masyarakat. Pada 22 April 2018, kawasan ini diresmikan sebagai kampung budaya dengan nama Kampoeng Heritage Kajoetangan.


Sejarah panjangnya tercermin dalam beragam arsitektur rumah yang masih bertahan hingga kini. Rumah-rumah dari periode 1910–1930-an memperlihatkan pengaruh Indische Empire Style, sedangkan Rumah Jengki mencerminkan perkembangan arsitektur pascakemerdekaan pada 1950–1960-an. Selain nilai arsitekturnya, rumah-rumah tersebut menyimpan kisah keluarga, tradisi, dan aktivitas ekonomi masyarakat, seperti usaha pembuatan sandal klompen kayu.


Kini, Kampoeng Heritage Kayutangan bukan sekadar kawasan bangunan bersejarah, melainkan ruang hidup yang mempertemukan warisan arsitektur, perdagangan, budaya, dan kehidupan sosial masyarakat dalam satu lingkungan yang khas.



History of Kampoeng Heritage Kayutangan

Kayutangan is one of Malang’s important commercial districts, which developed during the Dutch colonial period. Behind its main trading corridor lies Kampoeng Kayutangan, a residential area that has long served as a center of community life. On April 22, 2018, the area was officially designated as a cultural village under the name Kampoeng Heritage Kajoetangan.


Its long history is reflected in the diverse architectural styles of its surviving houses. Houses built between the 1910s and 1930s demonstrate the influence of the Indische Empire Style, while the Jengki House represents post-independence architectural development during the 1950s and 1960s. Beyond their architectural significance, these houses preserve stories of family life, traditions, and local economic activities, including the traditional craft of making wooden klompen sandals.


Today, Kampoeng Heritage Kayutangan is more than a collection of historic buildings. It is a living heritage area where architecture, commerce, culture, and community life come together to create a distinctive urban environment.
